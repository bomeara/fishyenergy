# Daily water temperature

This dataset contains mean daily water temperature for every day of the
year 2025 from the USGS gage on the Little River above Townsend, TN
(03497300).

## Usage

``` r
temp_nwis
```

## Format

A data frame with 365 rows and 2 columns:

- date:

  (date) dates from 1 January 2025 to 31 December 2025

- WT_mean:

  (numeric) mean daily water temperature in degrees C

## Source

National Water Information System.
